Who We Are

A Global Network of Excellence in IT Education

G-TEC Computer Education is an ISO-certified global institution and a trusted leader in IT education, skill development, and vocational training. Founded with a vision to democratize quality technical education, G-TEC has grown to become one of the most respected names in IT training.

With 25 years of excellence, G-TEC has built a strong network of over 800+ training institutions across more than 23 countries, empowering over 3.2 million students with industry-ready skills and internationally recognized certifications.

Our Anna Nagar, Madurai centre is a proud part of this global family — delivering the same world-class curriculum with personalized, hands-on training and dedicated placement support.

Our Story

25 Years of Growth & Impact

A journey of dedication, expansion, and transformation in IT education.

2000 — The Beginning

G-TEC Founded

G-TEC Computer Education was established with a mission to bridge the digital skills gap and provide affordable IT training across India.

2005 — ISO Certification

Internationally Recognized

G-TEC achieved ISO certification, making its certificates recognized globally by employers and institutions worldwide.

2010 — Global Expansion

Crossing Borders

G-TEC expanded its presence beyond India, establishing centres in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Africa — reaching students across continents.

2015 — 500+ Centres

Major Milestone Achieved

G-TEC crossed 500 active training centres and 1 million students trained — a testament to its global impact on IT education and employment.

2020 — Digital Evolution

Embracing the Future

G-TEC introduced advanced digital marketing, cloud computing, and AI-enabled courses, keeping pace with the 4th Industrial Revolution.

2025 — Silver Jubilee

25 Years Strong

G-TEC celebrates 25 years with 800+ centres in 23+ countries, 3.2 million+ students trained, and an unwavering commitment to shaping futures.

PC Vasantha Kumar — Leading with Passion

PC Vasantha Kumar is the Centre Director of G-TEC Computer Education, Anna Nagar, Madurai. With a deep passion for education and technology, he has been instrumental in building a centre that upholds the highest standards of G-TEC's global curriculum while nurturing local talent.

Under his leadership, G-TEC Anna Nagar has emerged as a preferred IT training destination in Madurai, empowering hundreds of students with job-ready skills, internationally recognized certifications, and dedicated placement support.

His commitment to student success, hands-on learning, and continuous improvement reflects the very values that G-TEC was founded upon. He personally oversees course quality, student counselling, and industry outreach — ensuring every student achieves their career goals.
